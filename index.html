Ann's Shelf

built by Ann

A pixel-art 3D shelf of records I love, plus a few things from my life.

How it works

  • Hover a record (or tap one on a phone) to pull it out and hear a preview
  • The things on the side shelves react too, many with sounds
  • Click once anywhere to turn sound on
  • Scroll to see more of the wall

Under the hood

Built with Three.js, Web Audio and Vite. Every sound effect is generated in code.
